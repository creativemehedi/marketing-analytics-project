# Marketing and Sales Performance Dashboard Mockup

This document outlines the conceptual design for a Power BI or Tableau dashboard to visualize the insights from the marketing and sales performance analysis. The goal is to provide a clear, interactive overview of marketing effectiveness and its impact on sales.

## Dashboard Overview

The dashboard will consist of several key sections, each focusing on a different aspect of the marketing and sales data. It will be designed for easy navigation and to allow users to drill down into details.

## Key Visualizations

### 1. Sales vs. Marketing Spend (Scatter Plots)

*   **Purpose:** To show the relationship between spending on each marketing channel (TV, Radio, Social Media) and the resulting sales.
*   **Chart Type:** Scatter Plot
*   **Details:**
    *   Three separate scatter plots: Sales vs. TV Budget, Sales vs. Radio Budget, Sales vs. Social Media Budget.
    *   Each point represents a data entry.
    *   Trend lines (linear regression lines) will be added to show the general relationship.
    *   Tooltips will display exact values for budget and sales on hover.

### 2. Sales Performance by Influencer Type (Bar Chart / Box Plot)

*   **Purpose:** To compare sales generated by different influencer categories (Mega, Macro, Nano, Micro).
*   **Chart Type:** Bar Chart (for average sales) or Box Plot (for distribution and outliers).
*   **Details:**
    *   X-axis: Influencer Type.
    *   Y-axis: Average Sales (Bar Chart) or Sales Distribution (Box Plot).
    *   Highlights which influencer types are most effective in driving sales.

### 3. Correlation Matrix Heatmap

*   **Purpose:** To visually represent the correlation coefficients between all numerical variables (TV, Radio, Social Media, Sales).
*   **Chart Type:** Heatmap
*   **Details:**
    *   Color intensity will indicate the strength and direction of the correlation.
    *   Annotations will display the exact correlation values.
    *   Provides a quick overview of which marketing channels have the strongest linear relationship with sales.

### 4. Actual vs. Predicted Sales (Line/Scatter Plot)

*   **Purpose:** To evaluate the accuracy of the predictive model by comparing actual sales values against the model's predictions.
*   **Chart Type:** Scatter Plot with a 45-degree reference line.
*   **Details:**
    *   X-axis: Actual Sales.
    *   Y-axis: Predicted Sales.
    *   Points close to the 45-degree line indicate good prediction accuracy.
    *   Allows for visual assessment of model performance.

## Key Metrics & KPIs (Displayed as Scorecards/Cards)

*   **Total Sales:** Overall sales generated.
*   **Average Sales per Influencer Type:** Average sales for each influencer category.
*   **Total Marketing Spend:** Sum of TV, Radio, and Social Media budgets.
*   **Return on Ad Spend (ROAS):** Total Sales / Total Marketing Spend (if applicable, assuming budgets are costs).
*   **Model R-squared:** Statistical measure indicating how well the regression model fits the data.
*   **Mean Squared Error (MSE):** Average of the squares of the errors (the average squared difference between the estimated values and the actual value).

## Interactive Elements

*   **Filters:** Allow users to filter data by Influencer Type.
*   **Drill-down:** Enable drilling down from overall sales to individual marketing channel performance.
*   **Tooltips:** Provide detailed information on hover for all charts.

## Dashboard Layout (Conceptual)

*   **Top Section:** Title, Key Metrics (scorecards for Total Sales, Total Marketing Spend, ROAS, R-squared).
*   **Middle Section (Left):** Sales vs. TV/Radio/Social Media Spend scatter plots.
*   **Middle Section (Right):** Sales Performance by Influencer Type (Bar/Box Plot).
*   **Bottom Section (Left):** Correlation Matrix Heatmap.
*   **Bottom Section (Right):** Actual vs. Predicted Sales plot.

This mockup provides a blueprint for building an insightful and user-friendly dashboard that effectively communicates the findings of the marketing and sales analysis.
